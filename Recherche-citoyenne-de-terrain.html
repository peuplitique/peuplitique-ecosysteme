Recherche Citoyenne du Terrain

Comprendre le terrain biologique à travers une recherche ouverte, citoyenne et mondiale, fondée sur la connaissance, le débat et la participation collective.

Pourquoi une recherche citoyenne du terrain ?

Les mécanismes biologiques fondamentaux concernent l'ensemble de l'humanité. Ils dépassent les frontières géographiques, économiques et idéologiques.

  • Rendre les connaissances accessibles à tous.
  • Développer une compréhension collective du vivant.
  • Ouvrir la recherche aux citoyens.
  • Favoriser la transparence des savoirs.
  • Créer un espace mondial de réflexion et de débat.
  • Contribuer à une gouvernance fondée sur la connaissance.
  • Expliquer simplement les mécanismes biologiques.
  • Ouvrir la recherche à tous les citoyens.
  • Créer un espace mondial de délibération.
  • Éviter la confiscation du savoir.
  • Développer une gouvernance fondée sur la connaissance.

    Citizen Field Research

    Understanding the biological terrain through open, citizen-driven and global research based on knowledge, debate and collective participation.

    Why Citizen Field Research?

    The fundamental mechanisms of living systems concern all societies. They are universal and transcend political, economic and ideological boundaries.

    • Make knowledge accessible to everyone.
    • Develop a collective understanding of living systems.
    • Open research to citizens.
    • Promote transparency of knowledge.
    • Create a global space for reflection and debate.
    • Contribute to governance based on knowledge.

    The 6 Areas of Citizen Research

    These areas are used to observe and understand the biological terrain. They are non-medical, non-prescriptive and non-partisan.

    1. Conductivity

    Study of biological microcurrents and their role in the coherence of living signals.

    2. Ionic Stability

    Analysis of the balance between major ions: Na+, K+, Ca²+, and Cl−.

    3. Biocompatibility

    Analysis of interactions between biological tissues and their environment.

    4. Inflammation

    Understanding inflammatory mechanisms and their impact on the overall balance of the terrain.

    5. Hormonal Regulation

    Analysis of hormonal variations and their influence on the overall stability of the biological system.

    6. T Lymphocytes

    Observation of the role of the immune system and T lymphocytes as central regulators of many biological processes.

    Integration into a Global Assembly Governance

    Peuplitique becomes a global coordination space where citizens can:

    • Understand terrain mechanisms.
    • Propose research directions.
    • Discuss societal implications.
    • Co-create ethical frameworks.
    • Participate in global governance of living systems.

    This governance is not a traditional political governance model. It is based on knowledge, transparency and citizen participation.

    Guiding Principle

    The priority is not disease but the understanding of the fundamental mechanisms of the biological terrain:

    1. Ions
    2. Electrochemical Signals
    3. T Lymphocytes
    4. Inflammation
    5. Hormones
    6. Observed Manifestations

    Integration within the Ecosystem

    Citizen Field Research contributes to the production of open and accessible knowledge for everyone.

    Its work feeds the Global Terrain Governance, which transforms knowledge into collective orientations, ethical frameworks and citizen coordination mechanisms.
